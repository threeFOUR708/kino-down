Questions

Why does Android show a warning?

Apps shared outside the Play Store always get this message. Tap "Install anyway" to continue.

How do I update the app?

Open the app. When a new version exists, a popup appears with an Update button. You never need to uninstall.

Do I need an account?

No. You only sign up to buy Premium.

What happens after the 5 free days?

You move to Basic: up to 1080p and 10 downloads a day. Premium removes the limit and unlocks higher quality, and Premium+ also removes the ads.

Where do my downloads go?

Videos go to Movies/KINO DOWN, music to Music/KINO DOWN and images to Pictures/KINO DOWN, so they show up in your gallery.

A site stopped working. What now?

Open the app, go to Home and tap Update downloader. Sites change often and this refreshes the downloader.

How do I install it on Windows?

Download the Windows file, open it and follow the installer. If Windows SmartScreen warns, tap More info → Run anyway. Sign in with the same account to keep your plan.

Is it legal?

Only download content you own or have permission to save. You are responsible for what you download.
